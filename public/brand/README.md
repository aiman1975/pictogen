# Toonaro brand

- `toonaro-wordmark.svg`, `toonaro-wordmark-white.svg`: the name, its two o's drawn as eyes looking
  right. The letters are Nunito Black (SIL OFL 1.1) as outlines, made by `scripts/brand/wordmark.py`.
- `toonaro-mascot.svg`: Toonaro, the toon-maker (round, orange, a blue beret, a pencil held up like a
  wand). It waves, bobs and blinks by itself, also as an `<img>`; still for viewers who ask for less motion.
- `../icons/icon.svg`: the app icon, the logo's two eyes and smile on orange to rose. The PNG icons
  are made from it by `scripts/brand/icons.cjs`.

Colours: orange `#fb923c` to rose `#f43f5e` (the icon), body orange `#fb923c`, beret blue `#3b82f6`,
ink `#1e1b4b`.

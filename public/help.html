How to use Toonaro

Toonaro turns a sentence into a picture. This page shows what you can write, and everything you can do with the picture afterwards. Every grey-blue example opens in the studio, so you can try it straight away.

1Getting started

1TypeWrite what you want to see in the box at the top, like you would tell a friend.
2DrawPress ✨ Draw (or Enter). The picture appears in a second.
3Make it yoursMove things around, try another style, then save it, print it, or make a video.

Not sure what to write? Open ✨ Ideas to try under the box and click one.

a girl with a balloon and her dog in the park three cats under a tree at night

2Writing a good sentence

Simple sentences work best. Name the things you want to see, then add details.

You want…Write, for example
Several things"a girl, a dog and a tree"
How many"three apples", "5 ducks", "a dozen eggs" (up to 12 of one thing)
Colours and sizes"a red car", "a huge tree", "a tiny mouse"
Feelings"a happy boy", "a sad girl", "a surprised man", "a laughing baby", "an angry cat"
Movement"waves", "jumps", "dances", "walks", "runs", "flies", "points", "cheers", "sleeps"
A place"in a classroom", "in the kitchen", "in the office", "in her bedroom", "in the park", "in the forest", "in the city", "at the beach", "in the desert", "in the mountains", "in space", "under the sea", "on the farm", "in the jungle", "at the castle", "in autumn"
Weather and time"at night", "at sunset", "in the rain", "in the snow"
Something in the hands"a woman holds a phone", "a girl with a balloon", "she reads a book", "a boy hugs a cat"
Someone talkingPut the words in quotes: a boy says "Hello!". A speech bubble appears and the mouth moves. Words in CAPITALS with "!" get a shouting bubble.
Looking at someone"a boy and a girl look at each other", "a girl waves at us"

💡 If Toonaro doesn't know a word, it says so under the box and suggests close words. Click a suggestion to use it. Small spelling mistakes ("elefant") are forgiven.

3People and characters

Everyone is drawn in the same style, so they belong together in one picture:

  • People: boy, girl, man, woman, baby, grandpa, grandma
  • Jobs: teacher, doctor, nurse, chef, police officer, firefighter, farmer, astronaut
  • Story characters: superhero, princess, prince, king, queen, pirate, wizard, witch, fairy, robot, scientist, a wise owl (the owl teacher), a little dragon, an idea buddy (a glowing lightbulb)
  • Thoughts: "a boy thinks about a cake", "she dreams of a castle", "a girl misses her grandma", "a girl has an idea": what they think of shows in a thought bubble over their head.
  • Groups: "children", "kids", "friends", "a team", "the family", "my grandparents" become a mix of people. "five kids" gives five.
  • Doing things together: "a boy sits on a chair", "grandma sits on the sofa and reads a book", "a boy and his grandma sit on a bench in the park", "a girl rides a bike", "a boy and a girl hold hands", "a girl hugs her grandma", "a woman sits on the grass". In a story with "then": "a boy walks in, then sits on the chair" walks over and sits down; "then hugs her grandma" walks up for a hug.
  • Feelings: happy, laughing, sad, surprised, angry, confused, scared, sleepy, excited, in love ("loves her dog"), shy. A little sign shows over the head: "!", "?", a heart, Zzz, sparkles. Feelings can change in a story: "a girl is scared, then a puppy runs to her, then she is excited".
  • Their face and hair: "a girl with big eyes and curly red hair", "a bald man with glasses", "a freckled boy with spiky hair", "a woman with a ponytail", "a boy with a round face". Hair: short, spiky, curly, afro, pigtails, bun, ponytail, long, bob, bald. And their body: "a man with a big belly and a long beard", "a chubby boy", "a skinny girl with long legs", "a bearded man", "a grandpa with a moustache".
  • What they wear: "a girl in a purple dress", "a man wearing a red coat", "a boy with curly black hair", "a blonde woman", "a red-haired girl".
  • Animals, things and more: over 3,000 pictures. Look them up in 📚 All pictures at the bottom of the studio, and click one to add it.

a teacher reads a book to children in a classroom a princess and a prince dance an astronaut waves in space a fish swims under the sea a girl diver waves at an octopus under the sea a knight rides a unicorn at the castle a friendly monster and a ghost dance at night

Places move too: stars twinkle and a shooting star crosses a clear night sky, bubbles rise and seaweed sways under the sea, leaves fall in autumn, flags wave on a castle, a planet floats in space.

4Pictures that move

Every picture moves a little: people blink and breathe, trees sway. To make things happen one after another, use then:

a boy walks in, then waves, then a dog runs to him a girl walks toward us, then says "Hello!", then walks away

  • At the same time: "while", "meanwhile": "a boy walks in, then while he waves, a dog runs to him".
  • How long: "waves for 3 seconds", "dances for a while".
  • The camera: "zoom in on the dog", "zoom out", "the camera follows the girl".
  • On the way: someone walking, running, driving, riding, flying or swimming (and nothing standing beside them) - the place goes by behind them, the far hills slowly, the near grass fast: "a boy runs in the forest by the mountains", "a car drives in the city". Going up or down, the land slides away and clouds stream past: "a rocket flies up into the sky", "a girl falls from the sky" (the ground comes up to land on), "a diver dives deeper under the sea".

Under the picture, the 🎬 Timeline shows every step. Click a step to change who does what, move steps earlier or later, add steps with ＋ Step, or drag a step's edge to make it longer. Ctrl+Z undoes a change.

The 🔊 Sound button turns sound on or off: footsteps, little voices ("ooh", "a-ha!") and the place's sounds. What people say stays silent, in their speech bubbles.

5Changing the picture

  • Move things: drag anything in the picture where you want it.
  • Click a thing to get its toolbar: ➕ Bigger, ➖ Smaller, ↔ Flip, ⬆ Front / ⬇ Back, 🗑 Delete, its mood (😊 😃 😮 😢 😉 😠 🤔) and a motion (talk, jump, dance, cheer, fly…).
  • Keyboard: arrow keys nudge the selected thing; Delete removes it.
  • ↩ Put back puts everything back where Toonaro placed it.
  • 🎲 Try another draws the same sentence again, a little differently. Different colours mixes up clothes, skin and fruit colours.

8Labelled diagrams and quizzes

Choose 🏷 Labelled Diagram, or just write the topic in an ordinary picture. Ready-made topics:

the parts of a plant parts of a tree the human body the water cycle inside a volcano layers of the Earth the solar system life cycle of a butterfly

  • Rename a label: click it, type, press Enter. ↺ Original labels brings them back.
  • 🙈 Blank labels (quiz): the labels become numbered lines, with a word bank underneath.
  • Vocabulary: any other words become pictures with their names, e.g. a cat, a dog, a cow and a pig. Blank them for a "write the word" exercise.

9Worksheets to print

With any picture on screen, click 🖨 Worksheet. You get an A4 page with a title, name and date lines, what to do, the picture and an activity:

  • ✏️ Write about the picture: handwriting lines.
  • 🔢 Count and write: "How many apples?" boxes, and "How many altogether?". Try 3 apples and 2 bananas.
  • 🏷 Label the diagram: blank labels and a word bank.
  • 🖍 Colour it in.

Toonaro picks the best fit, and you can choose another. Change the title and the instructions if you like. Counting and labelling sheets come with an answer key on a second page (untick it if you don't want it). Then click ⬇ Download PDF or 🖨 Print.

10Story clips

  • Ready-made: choose 🎬 Story Clip, pick a kind (kids' stories, science, news, how-to), then a story, and press play. Choose wide, phone (9:16) or square. Next to 🔊 Sound, 🎚 picks the sounds: Playful (cartoon footsteps, boings and voices), Clean (subtle, for explainers and presentations) or Auto (by the style and the kind of clip). Footsteps go by the walker's size, phones ring, the school bell rings, magic sparkles, and each place has its own quiet sound (birds, waves, crickets at night, bubbles under the sea). 🎵 turns the music on or off, and the list next to it picks the music: the kind's own, or a mood (happy, calm, adventure, mystery, under the sea, bedtime, celebration…).
  • Write a whole story: in 🎬 Story Clip, write or paste your story in the big box, one part per line (or just sentences), and press ✨ Draw (or Ctrl + Enter). Each line becomes a scene with your words as its caption. Name your characters ("a little girl called Mia", "Rex the dog", "Grandma Rose") and they look the same all the way through; "she", "he" and "they" are understood. Where and when it happens carries on from scene to scene ("near a big forest", "at night", "it started to rain"). English only for now.
  • Check how it was read: under the clip, 📖 How I read your story lists the characters and, for each scene, your sentence and what is drawn for it. Change what a scene shows and press Enter. ★ Keep makes a character from the story one of your characters, to pick their colours or add a photo face.
  • An explainer from an outline: instead of a story, write an outline and you get a clip of cards (like presentation videos, no voice needed), each on screen long enough to read: # Title (and a line under it for the subtitle), then for each card ## Headline and its points - …, which appear one by one with a picture beside them. A headline starting with a number (## 7% a year) shows the number big; ## Saving vs Spending compares two sides (write each point as - left | right); numbered points (1. 2.) become steps; > words — who said them is a quote; ## Recap ticks its points off. Show: coins and a chart under a headline picks its picture; Scene: a teacher points at the board | caption puts an acted scene between the cards. A quiz: ## Quiz: question? with its answers as - [x] the right one and - [ ] a wrong one; after a moment to think, the right answer turns green. Try 📋 Explainer from an outline under How-To Guides; then 📋 Your cards under the clip lets you change any card's words, kind or picture, move cards, remove or add them, and make the clip again. Next to Export Video, pick Fade or Cut for how one scene follows the next.
  • From your notes: paste notes or an article in the story box and press 📝 Notes → explainer. It picks the main points from your own sentences (shortened, nothing made up), finds steps, numbers, quotes and comparisons, adds a recap, and writes the outline in the box; the clip is made from it. Check it, change it there or in 📋 Your cards, and make it again. ↺ Back to my notes brings your notes back.
  • Auf Deutsch: outlines and notes can be in German. Use ## Zusammenfassung, ## Frage:, Bild:, Szene:, ## Sparen gegen Ausgeben; paste German notes and press 📝 Notes → explainer - the language is recognised by itself.
  • Each scene's card: under each sentence, a card shows what the scene is made of: 👤 Figures (who, and how they feel), 📦 Objects (＋ adds someone or something to the scene, with suggestions as you type: one of your characters, a teacher, a dog, three balloons…; × leaves one out), 🏞️ Place (where, when and the weather: pick another place, one of your places, another time or weather for just this scene; ↺ goes back to what your words say), 💭 Feeling (the scene's mood), 📷 Camera (Auto lets the director choose: close on whoever speaks, a two-shot when two talk, wide first at a new place, pushing in when tense, pulling back at the end, a shake for a crash; or pick Steady, Wide, Close on someone, Follow someone, Push in, Pull back, a pan, a low angle where figures loom, a high angle where they look small, or a shake), 🎨 Look (this scene in another style than the clip: a stickman explainer moment in a flat video), 🎬 Action (what happens, in order: ✎ Steps lets you change who does what, to whom, add, remove and reorder steps, and make a step happen at the same time as the one before; ✓ uses them, ↺ goes back to your words) and 💬 Message (what is said, what the board says: ✎ Words lets you change who says a line and its words, add or remove lines, and write on the board; ✓ uses them, ↺ next to "I'll draw" goes back to your words).
  • The mood of a scene: each scene can feel tense, joyful, sad, calm or mysterious. It is read from your words ("Suddenly…", "worried", "Well done!") or picked in the card's 💭 Feeling list. The mood changes the light (dark edges when tense, a warm glow when joyful, paler colours when sad), moves the camera slowly (pushing in when tense, easing back when sad), plays a short sound as the scene starts, and in a video the music turns to the mood's tune while the mood lasts.
  • Read it aloud: the 🗣 button in the player reads each caption with your device's own voice, and waits for it before the next scene. (Videos are made without the voice.)
  • Your own story: in 🌄 Picture, make a scene and click ＋ Add this scene under Your clip. Make the next scene and add it too. Give each one a caption, put them in order, then click ▶ Play clip.
  • Save it: as a video (🎬 Export Video) or as a web page that plays anywhere, even offline (💾 Export Clip).
  • Make it a picture book: click 📖 Picture Book. You get a cover with your title, "by …" and a picture, one page for each scene with its caption in big letters, and "The End". Pick the colours, then download the PDF or print it (landscape A4). Each picture shows the moment when the most is happening.

11Your own characters

Give your story a cast that looks the same in every picture. Under Your characters, click ＋ New character: give them a name (e.g. Mia), pick what they are (a boy, a girl, a man, a woman, a grandma, a grandpa, a baby, a dog or a cat), and pick their clothes, hair and skin colours (fur for pets). Now write the name in your sentences: Mia walks in, then waves at Rex.

Mia then looks the same in every scene, in every scene of a clip, and in all three panels of a comic strip. Click ✎ to change her look; every new picture uses it. Under 🙂 Face, hair & body you can also pick her hair style, face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, cheeks and beard, her body (slim, round, a big belly), height, and long or short legs and arms, and add freckles, eyelashes or glasses. Under 🎒 Things to wear give her a diving mask, goggles, a hard hat, a crown, a cap, headphones, a scarf, a tie, an air tank, a backpack, a cape, wings, flippers or boots.

Anyone can wear these in words too: a girl in a red cape and a crown, a man with a diving mask and flippers, a boy wearing a hard hat. Some people come ready dressed: a diver (mask, air tank and flippers), a girl diver, a builder, a hiker, a tourist, a DJ, a swimmer.

Want a real face? Click 📷 From a photo, choose a photo, fit the face into the oval and give it a name, e.g. Tom. Tom gets the face from the photo, and his mouth moves when he talks: Tom says "Hello!" to a dog.

💡 Without your own characters, a colour said once stays in a clip: if scene 1 says a girl in a purple dress, "the girl" in the next scenes wears it too.
🔒 Names and photos never leave your device. They are kept in this browser only. Use ✕ next to a character to remove it.

Your own places

Under 🧩 Your figures, click ＋ Make it move to bring a drawing to life. 1 Picture: draw it with the pen (colours, sizes, an eraser) or bring in a picture (a drawing, or a photo cut out on a clear background). 2 Parts and dots: pick what a part is (a leg, an arm, a head, a tail, or another part), draw a loop round it on the picture, and drag its red dot to where it bends (a leg: where it joins at the top). Two legs walk like a person, four like a dog, no legs hop and wobble as a whole. 3 Face: pick Eyes and click where each eye goes (one or two), then Mouth and click where the mouth goes; drag them to move them, pick their sizes, add rosy cheeks. The figure then blinks, talks with its mouth moving, and shows how it feels (happy, sad, surprised, laughing, cross, sleepy, in love…); a mouth already in your picture is covered by the new one. Marked inside the head's loop, the face turns with the head. 4 Moves: a part can also move on its own all the time (swing, nod, shake, bounce, spin): pick it, pick the move and drag on the picture to set which way and how far. 5 Name: give it a name (e.g. Mr Table) and a size, try it with Walk, Run, Dance, Jump or Wave, and keep it. Then use the name: Mr Table walks to the door, then Mr Table dances, in scenes and stories, with steps, moods and the camera like any figure. The picture and the name stay in this browser.

Under 🏞️ Your places, click ＋ New place. Pick where it is (a meadow, a farm, a forest, the beach, under the sea, the desert, space, a classroom, a kitchen…), the time of day, the weather, and what else is there: mountains or a castle behind, clouds drifting, twinkling stars, a shooting star, fireflies, butterflies, bubbles or falling leaves. Give it a name, e.g. Grandma's farm, and use the name: a girl waves at Grandma's farm. In a story the place carries on until the story goes somewhere else.

A room can be made your own too: pick the walls' and the floor's colour, what is in it (a board, a window, a clock, a shelf, a plant, a map, a picture, bunting, a lamp), a chalkboard or a whiteboard, and what is written on it.

Write on the board in a sentence: a teacher points at the board that says "2 + 2 = 4", a girl writes "Hello class!" on the whiteboard.

💡 Say at night or in the rain to change your place for one scene: Tom sleeps at Grandma's farm at night.

13On a phone

  • Swipe the rows of choices sideways to see them all.
  • Tap a thing in the picture: its toolbar opens at the bottom of the screen. Tap ✓ Done to close it.
  • Drag with one finger to move things.

Install it as an app (works offline)

Once opened, Toonaro keeps everything it needs on your device, so it also works with no internet. You can add it as an app with its own icon:

  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click ⬇ Install app at the top of the studio (or the install icon in the address bar).
  • Android: tap ⬇ Install app, or the browser menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Updates arrive on their own: the next time you open it with internet, the new version is kept for offline use too.

14Your privacy

  • Everything is drawn on your own device. What you type and make is not sent anywhere.
  • No account, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No AI service in the cloud: Toonaro draws every picture itself.
  • Your saved scenes and characters stay in this browser. Clearing the browser's data removes them.

15Good to know

  • Keep sentences short and clear: one scene per sentence works best.
  • Some actions aren't drawn yet: riding a bike, sitting on a chair, holding hands. The bike or chair is drawn next to the person.
  • A name like "Tom" needs a character from Your characters; otherwise write "a boy".
  • A colouring page shows the first thing in your sentence.
  • Toonaro is a preview. If something looks wrong, try other words, or click 🎲 Try another.

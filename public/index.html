How it works

No sign-up, nothing to install. It works on a computer, a tablet or a phone.

Type what you want to see"a teacher reads a book to children in a classroom". Simple words work best.
Press DrawThe picture appears in a second. Move things around, pick a style, change a face.
Save, print or shareAs a picture, a video, a comic, or a worksheet to print, with an answer key.

What you can make

For lessons, birthday cards, bedtime stories, presentations, or just for fun.

🖼️

Pictures in 23 styles

Cartoon, watercolour, anime, crayon, chalkboard, pixel art and more. Drag things around to make it yours.

🎬

Little cartoons with sound

"A boy walks in, then waves": people move, talk with speech bubbles, and you can save a video.

💬

Comic strips

Three panels with your own words in the bubbles.

🏷️

Labelled diagrams

The parts of a plant, the water cycle, the solar system... Rename the labels, or hide them for a quiz.

🖨️

Worksheets to print

Write about it, count and write, label it, colour it in. As a PDF, with an answer key.

🙂

Your own characters

Give a figure a name, or even your face from a photo. It stays on your device.

🔒

Private, the way it should be

  • Everything is drawn on your own device. What you type and make is never sent anywhere.
  • No account, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No AI service in the cloud: every picture is drawn by PictoGen itself, so you can use what you make.
  • Photos for your own characters stay in your browser.
